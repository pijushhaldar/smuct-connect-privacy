Privacy Policy for SMUCT Connect

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

SMUCT Connect is the official community information application associated with Sarbojanin Milan Utsav Charitable Trust (SMUCT), Sector 37C & D, Gurugram, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SMUCT Connect mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

SMUCT Connect does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The current version of the application does not directly collect or store:

  • Name or profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Photos or videos submitted by users
  • User-generated content
  • Payment card or bank account information

2. Information Displayed by the App

The application provides information relating to SMUCT activities, including Durga Puja schedules, cultural events, special events, announcements, contact information and community activities.

Information displayed in the application may include names and telephone numbers of designated SMUCT organizers or event contacts. This information is provided for community and event-support purposes.

3. Telephone Calls

The Puja Help Desk section allows users to tap a telephone number to initiate a call using the phone's built-in calling application.

SMUCT Connect does not record, monitor or store the contents of telephone calls made through this feature.

4. External Applications and Websites

Certain features of the application may open external applications or websites.

For example, the Join Us section may allow users to open a supported UPI or payment application to make a voluntary contribution. The application does not collect or store the user's payment credentials or banking information.

The application may also provide links to external social-media or other websites. Once you leave SMUCT Connect and access an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Payments

SMUCT Connect does not process or store payment card, bank account or UPI credentials within the application.

Where a payment or contribution option is provided, the transaction is initiated through an external payment or UPI application selected by the user. Any information submitted during such a transaction is handled by the relevant payment service provider according to its own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

SMUCT Connect is intended as a general community information application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Since the application does not require user registration or directly collect personal information, children can access the informational content without creating an account.

7. Data Security

Because the current version of SMUCT Connect does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to the application, there is no user account database maintained by the application.

We nevertheless take reasonable measures to maintain the security and integrity of the application and its published information.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version of SMUCT Connect may interact with external applications or websites when the user chooses to use features such as telephone calling, UPI/payment applications or external social-media links.

These external services operate independently and may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of SMUCT Connect, please use the contact information provided on the SMUCT Connect Google Play Store listing.
